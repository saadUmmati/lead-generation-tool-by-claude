How Lead Finder works

1. What it does

Enter a business type and a location. The tool searches Google Maps (and, optionally, OpenStreetMap) for matching businesses, visits each one's website for public emails and social profile links, checks those emails for basic validity, scores each lead, and gives you a downloadable list.

2. While it runs

The log panel shows every step live: discovery starting and finishing, then each business being enriched one at a time as it completes.

3. Stopping early

Click "Stop & Save What's Found" at any point, in any phase — everything discovered and/or enriched so far is saved to your download, nothing is lost.

4. Email validation

Each email gets a confidence rating: High (valid syntax, real mail server, not a generic role account), Medium (valid but role-based like info@, or unverifiable), Low (disposable domain or role-based with no confirmation), Invalid (bad syntax or no mail server found). This is a local, free check — no data is sent to any paid verification service.

5. Quality score

A transparent 0–100 score per business, built from: website present, phone present, email confidence, number of social profiles found, and review rating/count. It's a sorting aid, not a guarantee.

6. About social profiles

Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp require a login to view most content, so this tool can only capture the public link to each profile found on a business's own website — not private data behind a login.

7. Everything is free & local

No paid APIs, no accounts required, no data leaves your computer except to the websites/services being queried (Google Maps via gosom, and optionally OpenStreetMap).
